Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Summary. TempQR is built with a privacy-first approach. We do not require accounts, we do not use tracking cookies, and we do not sell your data.

Accounts
No signup required (email only for Pro).
Tracking cookies
None. We do not track your behavior across the web.
Purpose
Generate expiring links, QR codes, and process Pro upgrades.

1. Information We Process

When you use TempQR, we process the following information:

  • Link Data: The target URL you submit and the expiration settings you choose, required to generate your link and QR code.
  • Pro Account Data: If you purchase a Pro plan, we store the email address you provided during checkout to unlock your features.
  • Technical Data: Like all websites, our servers automatically receive standard technical data (such as IP address and browser type) to ensure security, prevent abuse, and maintain service stability.

2. What We DO NOT Do

  • We do not track who opens your links or their physical location.
  • We do not use third-party advertising trackers or sell your data.
  • We do not require passwords or store sensitive personal profiles.

3. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party providers to run TempQR:

  • Freemius: Handles all payment processing and subscription management securely. We do not see or store your credit card information.
  • Hosting & Database Providers: We use secure cloud infrastructure to host the website and store link data.

4. Data Retention

Expiring links stop working automatically after the time you select. We do not keep a permanent history of your generated links. Technical logs are retained only for a limited time for security and debugging purposes.

5. Contact

If you have any privacy questions or concerns, please contact us at support@tempqr.com.
