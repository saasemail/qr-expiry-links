Security at TempQR

How we protect your links, data, and privacy.

Our Commitment. TempQR is built with a security-first mindset. We understand that sharing links often involves sensitive information, and we take the responsibility of protecting that data seriously.

1. Infrastructure Security

  • HTTPS Everywhere: All traffic to and from TempQR is encrypted using modern TLS protocols.
  • Strict Security Headers: We use Content Security Policy (CSP) and other HTTP headers to prevent Cross-Site Scripting (XSS) and other web vulnerabilities.
  • DDoS Protection: Our hosting infrastructure automatically mitigates malicious traffic and denial-of-service attacks.

2. Data Protection

  • No Passwords: We do not use traditional accounts or store user passwords. Pro access is unlocked securely via email verification.
  • Payment Security: All transactions are processed by Freemius, a PCI-DSS compliant payment provider. We never see or store your credit card information.
  • Link Privacy: Your original destination URLs are hidden behind our temporary redirects and are never exposed publicly.

3. Anti-Abuse

To keep the platform safe for everyone, we employ automated systems to detect and block the creation of harmful links, including phishing attempts, malware distribution, and links to known malicious domains.

4. Reporting a Vulnerability

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability on TempQR, please contact us immediately at support@tempqr.com. We appreciate your help in keeping our platform secure.
