FAQ

Quick answers about expiring links and QR codes.

How does TempQR work?

You paste a URL, choose an expiration time, and TempQR generates a temporary shareable link and a QR code. Once the time is up, the link automatically stops working.

Is TempQR free to use?

TempQR operates on a freemium model. You can create unlimited free links that expire after 1 hour, without any signup. If you need custom durations (up to 30 days) or advanced features like Link Usage Limits, you can upgrade to Pro.

What are the Pro pricing options?

We offer three options:
• Annual Plan: $71.90/year (Save 25%).
• Monthly Plan: $7.99/month.
• Single Link: $2.99 one-time payment for one custom link (no subscription).

What is the "Link Usage Limit" (Max Opens) feature?

This Pro feature allows you to set a maximum number of times a link or QR code can be opened (e.g., limit to 1, 5, 100, or any number you need). Once the limit is reached, the link dies. Link previews from bots (like WhatsApp or Viber) are not counted.

How do I unlock Pro features without an account?

We hate passwords as much as you do. When you subscribe, your email address becomes your key. After payment, simply click on "Already purchased?" on the website, enter the email you used at checkout, and Pro features will unlock instantly on that device.

I cleared my cache or changed devices. How do I restore my Pro access?

Your email address acts as your key — there are no passwords to remember. If you switch devices, use incognito mode, or clear your browser cache, simply click on "Already purchased?" on the main page and enter the exact email address you used at checkout. Your Pro status will unlock instantly.

What happens after the link expires or reaches its limit?

The expiring link stops redirecting to the target URL. People clicking or scanning it will see a clean message that the link has expired or reached its maximum number of opens.

Can I edit or extend an existing expiring link?

No. TempQR is designed for privacy and simplicity. If you need to change a link, please generate a new one.

How does TempQR handle my privacy and data?

TempQR is built with a strict privacy-first approach. We do not use tracking cookies, we do not track your IP address, and we do not sell your data to third parties. Your original links remain completely hidden behind your TempQR link.

We do not track who opens your links or where they are located. If you use the "Link Usage Limit" (Max Opens) feature, we only store a simple, anonymous counter (e.g., "3 opens remaining"). We have no way of knowing who those 3 opens belong to.

Why does the link preview sometimes look different in chat apps?

Chat apps cache previews aggressively. Some apps show an image card, others show only a link. Sending the same link later may still show the old preview until the app's cache refreshes.

How do I contact support?

You can reach us at support@tempqr.com. We usually respond within 24 hours.
